# Geological Background

Antioquia is one of Colombia's historically important gold provinces. Mineralization styles include orogenic gold, intrusion-related systems, epithermal signatures, and structurally controlled vein systems. Exploration programs commonly combine lithology, structure, alteration mapping, geophysics, and multi-element geochemistry.

## Pathfinder Logic

- Au: economic target variable, highly skewed and nugget-prone.
- Ag: precious-metal association, useful in epithermal and polymetallic systems.
- As and Sb: classic gold pathfinders, often linked to arsenopyrite, stibnite, and hydrothermal fluids.
- Hg and Te: volatile and telluride pathfinders, useful in epithermal or intrusion-related contexts.
- Bi, W, Mo: intrusion-related or reduced-intrusion affinity indicators.
- Cu, Pb, Zn: base-metal association, sulfide halos, and polymetallic zoning.
- Fe, S: sulfide and alteration proxy.
- Mn: weathering and lithogeochemical context.

## Why ML Matters

Traditional thresholding can miss multivariate weak signals. Machine learning can combine subtle pathfinder enrichment, spatial context, alteration proxies, and non-linear interactions. The output should still be interpreted geologically and used to prioritize field validation, not to replace field geology.
